# 9e24ba7 — /plan [play-account-type]: the Play developer account settled as an organization account, because a personal one puts the user's home address on every subscriber's receipt

The capture held a choice that could not be undone by editing a setting later, and it was raised
because the user asked whether the paid tier depends on their business being set up. It does not —
both Google Play account types can sell subscriptions, so a registered business is not required. What
does depend on it is the account type, which is why this was a decision rather than a step.

The user settled it: an organization account, registered to their business. The deciding fact is what
the account publishes rather than what it costs — the payments profile's legal name and address appear
on customer receipts, and on a personal account those are the individual's own name and home address,
sent to every subscriber. The personal route was cheaper and faster and lost on that alone.

The research behind it was filed as `workshop/resources/research/google-play-organization-account-requirements.md`
with its index line, because it had to be read before the walkthrough could be written and would
otherwise have to be redone. It turned up two things Claude would have got wrong from memory: the
D-U-N-S number is free but takes up to 30 days, and an organization account also publishes a phone
number and requires a website.

**Queue changes:** [play-account-type] deleted, its facts folded into [play-console-subscription-product]
where they are used. That item's walkthrough rewritten for the organization path, with the D-U-N-S
request as its first step, and its `Blocked by:` moved from this capture to the new
[business-registration-for-play-account].
