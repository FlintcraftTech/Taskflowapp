profile's legal name and address are printed on every subscriber's receipt. That is the entire reason an
**organization** account was chosen over a personal one on 2026-09-03 — a personal account publishes the
individual's own name and home address to every subscriber. The D-U-N-S already held was issued to a
**sole trader**, which is not a separate legal entity: its registered name is Alex's own and its registered
address is whatever the registration carries. So using it would have published exactly what the account
type was chosen to avoid, while appearing to satisfy the decision.

former one she no longer occupies and is not free to publish. Proof of physical address could not be
supplied for it either. So "does Google accept a sole trader as an organization?" is not the open question
— a yes to it would change nothing about what gets printed. That correction is recorded because it was the
question this entry was first framed around.

[play-console-subscription-product]: the Play account is not opened on any identity whose published address
